## Creative decisions (Q&A)

### 1. Where to film unboxings?

**Answer:** **Both** — on the dock **and** inside the fish house.

- Use dock for open-air, lake backdrop and natural light.
- Use the fish house for cozy, “camp / ice house” energy and weather backup.

### 2. What gear to feature?

**Answer:** **Both** — full mix.

- **Fishing:** rods, lures, terminal tackle, mystery bait.
- **Hunting / field:** camo and related outdoor hunting gear (mixed with the fishing story so it feels like one northern trip, not two separate shows).

### 3. Kids’ role on camera?

**Answer:** **They do it all.**

- Open boxes, react, test mystery bait on camera.
- **B-roll / adventure beats:** haul gear or deliveries by **snowmobile** or **four-wheeler** (treat the run like “UPS showed up at the lake” — motion and stakes).

## Episode spine (working)

1. Arrival / lake establishing (dock or trail).
2. Unboxing — split: dock segment + fish house segment.
3. Kids test bait / gear; genuine reactions.
4. Payoff: riding out with gear (sled / ATV), optional quick fishing beat if you have the catch.

## Notes for production

- Prioritize safety and age-appropriate vehicle use; helmets, supervised operation, and local regs.
- Audio: wind on the dock vs. deadened space in the fish house — plan lav or dual setup if possible.
