## How money connects to PSP (keep it simple)

Focus on **three levers** — nothing else until one works:

1. **Retention** — players come back daily (garden, fashion, events, social loops).
2. **Monetization** — clear paid upgrades that feel fair (cosmetics, plot perks, speed-ups — not pay-to-win rage).
3. **Publish & iterate** — ship to Roblox, watch analytics, one improvement per week.

**Today's question for every prompt:** *Does this help someone stay longer or pay once without feeling cheated?*

If no → park it.
